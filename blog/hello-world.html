Hello, world: why I'm finally writing in public

18 Nov 2026 · 2 min read

After more than ten years in tech, from router firmware on OpenWRT to cloud engineering, integration platforms and now cloud architecture, I've collected a lot of notes. Most of them live in ADRs, wikis and chat threads that nobody outside the team will ever see. This site is where I'll share the parts that might be useful to others, mostly as notes to my future self.

What you'll find here

  • Cloud architecture: patterns that held up in production, and a few that didn't.
  • Infrastructure as code: AWS CDK, CloudFormation and Terraform in practice.
  • DevSecOps: encryption, access control and monitoring without slowing teams down.
  • Regulated IT and AI: data governance, Digital Product Passports, and using AI responsibly.
  • Side projects: home automation, AI tooling and other experiments.

Why now

Writing an Architecture Decision Record forces you to explain why, not just what. I've found the same is true for blog posts: if I can't explain something simply, I don't understand it well enough yet.

Small posts, real examples, no hype. I'll get things wrong sometimes, and I'm happy to be corrected.

Thanks for reading. If something here helps you, or you disagree, tell me.
