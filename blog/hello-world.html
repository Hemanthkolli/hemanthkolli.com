Hello, world: why I'm finally writing in public

18 Nov 2026 · 1 min read

I've worked with computers for over ten years, from home routers to big cloud systems. Along the way I've collected a lot of notes. This site is where I share the useful ones.

What you'll find here

  • Tinkering: smart home gadgets, a Raspberry Pi or two, and what I learn when things break.
  • AI, explained simply: what AI assistants can do for you, and how to use them carefully.
  • Tech for everyone: plain explainers, like how the internet works or how to stay safe online.
  • From my day job: now and then, practical notes for people who build software.

Why now

If I can't explain something simply, I don't understand it well enough yet. Writing helps me check.

Small posts, real examples, no hype. I'll get things wrong sometimes, and I'm happy to be corrected.

Thanks for reading. If something here helps you, or you disagree, tell me.
