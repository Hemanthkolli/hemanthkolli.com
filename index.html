01 · Approach

How I like to work

Three habits I keep coming back to, whatever the platform.

i.

Platforms teams want to use

Automated infrastructure as code, clear integration patterns and sensible defaults, so the easy path is also the safe one.

ii.

Compliance designed in

On regulatory platforms, traceability and data quality shape every decision. I'd rather plan for them early than retrofit them late.

iii.

Decisions on paper, hands on the code

Architecture Decision Records for the "why", and staying close enough to the code to know what the decision really costs.

02 · About

From the metal to the cloud

I started close to the hardware, writing router software on OpenWRT, then moved through IoT research and a master's in high-integrity systems into cloud engineering on AWS. Since then I've worked on integration platforms and APIs, and as product owner and solution architect on an EU regulatory data platform.

Today I work on the cloud infrastructure behind an integration platform. Each role taught me something the next one needed, and I keep learning from the people I work with.

Lately I'm exploring how AI can be used well in regulated environments, and writing down what I learn along the way. Away from the keyboard you'll find me on a badminton, football or padel court, at the snooker table, or planning the next trip.
