#about

I'm a cloud architect who likes staying close to the code. Over the years I've worked as a cloud engineer, on integration platforms and APIs, and as a product owner and solution architect on an EU regulatory data platform. Today I work on the cloud infrastructure behind an integration platform. Each role taught me something the next one needed.

What I enjoy most is helping build platforms that other teams find easy to use: automated infrastructure as code, clear integration patterns and sensible security defaults. Working on EU regulatory platforms has taught me how much compliance, traceability and data quality matter, and I keep learning from the people I work with.

I started close to the metal, writing router software on OpenWRT, then moved through IoT research into cloud and architecture. I see myself as a learner first: every project, team and conversation teaches me something, and I'm always happy to swap ideas. Away from the keyboard you'll find me playing badminton, football or padel, at the snooker table, or planning the next trip.
