01 · About

A bit about me

My way into tech started at home. My sibling and cousins were studying computer science, and growing up in India, the choice often felt like "engineer or doctor". So I followed them into engineering.

Slowly, curiosity took over. I wanted to know how things inside a computer really work. I started rooting Android phones and flashing software from different manufacturers, then moved on to routers. That path ended in my bachelor's thesis on Quality of Service in routers: how a router decides which traffic gets to go first.

Later I moved to Germany for a master's in High Integrity Systems. The name sounds grand, but the idea is simple: how do you build systems that people can trust, where a failure isn't an option? Think of the software in cars, planes or hospitals. I learned about safety-critical systems, IT security, distributed systems, cloud computing and IoT. For my thesis I researched smart jackets that could be controlled with hand gestures, using sensors woven into the fabric.

Germany has been home ever since. I still love taking things apart to see how they work, and writing things down helps me understand them better, so I keep notes here.

Away from the keyboard you'll find me on a badminton, football or padel court, at the snooker table, out running or cycling, or planning the next trip.
